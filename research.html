Political Commitments and Policy Reversals

Book

The Politics of Policy Reversals in Europe. Book manuscript in progress. The book examines why political actors reverse publicly stated commitments, and why some policy promises become unsustainable over time. It develops a theory of dimensional expansion, arguing that commitments often become unstable when implementation activates new material, institutional or normative pressures that were initially underweighted. Combining comparative analysis of policy reversals across the Netherlands, the United Kingdom and Germany (2010-2025) with original experimental evidence, the book explores how and why commitments shift across immigration, housing and climate policy.
